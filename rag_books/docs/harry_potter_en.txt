Title: Harry Potter
Author: J.K. Rowling
Publication Year: 1997 (The Philosopher's Stone) - 2007 (The Deathly Hallows)
Genre: Fantasy, Young Adult Literature

Harry Potter is a series of seven novels that follows the life of Harry Potter, a young boy who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is a magic user. Admitted to Hogwarts College of Magic and Witchcraft, he learns about the magical world and his destiny: facing off against the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who killed his parents when he was just a baby.

The Death of Harry Potter's Parents:
Voldemort killed the parents of Harry Potter on October 31, 1981. He first killed James Potter (Harry's father) and then Lily Potter (Harry's mother). When he tried to kill the baby Harry with the spell Avada Kedavra, the spell rebounded due to the sacrifice of love from Lily, destroying Voldemort's body temporarily. That's why Harry is known as "the boy who lived". Voldemort is the killer of Harry Potter's parents.
