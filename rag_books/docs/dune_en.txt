Title: Dune
Author: Frank Herbert
Publication Year: 1965
Genre: Science Fiction, Space Opera

Synopsis:
Set in a distant future of interstellar space where noble families control planets, the story follows Paul Atreides, whose family accepts control of the desert planet Arrakis, the only place in the universe where the valuable "spice" melange is produced. After a betrayal, Paul must survive in the desert and unite with the native Fremen people of Arrakis.

Paul Atreides, the young noble with special abilities, is the protagonist of the story. His mother, Lady Jessica, is a member of the Bene Gesserit order and his father, Duc Leto Atreides, leads the House of Atreides. Chani, a young Fremen who becomes Paul's companion, plays an important role in the plot as well. Stilgar, the leader of the Fremen tribe that accepts Paul, is also a key character. Baró Vladimir Harkonnen, the main antagonist and enemy of the Atreides family, drives the conflict forward. The Emperor Shaddam IV, the ruler of the known universe, provides the larger context for the story.

Books of the main saga:

2. The Messiah of Dune (1969)
3. Sons of Dune (1976)
4. Emperor of Dune (1981)
5. The Heirs of Dune (1984)
6. The House of the Wives (1985)

Frank Herbert's "Dune" is the most widely sold science fiction novel of all time. The book was inspired by Oregon sand dunes, and Arrakis was created based on them. The melange species is an allegory of oil and scarce natural resources. "Dune" was adapted to film by David Lynch in 1984 and by Denis Villeneuve in 2021 and 2024. It heavily influenced Star Wars, with Tatooine being a desert planet similar to Arrakis. The book won the Hugo and Nebula awards in 1966.

### The Night of the Shooting Star

On the night of the shooting star, everyone gathered around the television to watch the special programme. There were children running around the room, excited about the magic that was about to happen. As the clock struck seven in the evening, a hush fell over the city, and everyone stopped what they were doing to look up at the sky.

For a few moments, time seemed to stand still as the stars came out in all their glory. There were shooting stars streaking across the sky, leaving trails of light behind them. It was a sight that filled everyone with wonder and amazement.

As the night went on, people began to share stories about their dreams and aspirations. Some talked about their hopes of becoming famous, while others dreamt of travelling the world or starting their own business. Everyone felt inspired by the beauty of the night sky and the sense of possibility it created.

As the programme drew to a close, everyone gathered around the television once again to watch the final segment. There was a moment of silence as the screen flickered to black, and then suddenly, a message appeared on the screen. It read: "Dream big and never give up."

The audience erupted in cheers and applause, and everyone started to feel a sense of hope and excitement for what the future held. The night of the shooting star had been a magical experience that would be remembered for years to come.
