Title: The Lord of the Rings
Author: J.R.R. Tolkien
Year of Publication: 1954 (The Fellowship of the Ring), 1955 (The Two Towers and The Return of the King)
Genre: Epic fantasy

Synopsis:
The Lord of the Rings tells the story of Frodo Baggins, a hobbit who inherits the One Ring, a powerful object created by the Dark Lord Sauron to rule over all the peoples of Middle Earth. Frodo must embark on a dangerous journey to Mount Doom in Mordor to destroy the ring and save the world.

Frodo Baggins: The hobbit protagonist and carrier of the One Ring.
Gandalf: A mage and guide to the Fellowship of the Ring.
Aragorn: Heir to the throne of Gondor, also known as Strider.
Legolas: An elf archer and member of the Fellowship.
Gimli: A dwarf warrior and member of the Fellowship.
Sam Gamgee: Frodo's loyal friend and servant.
Gollum: A corrupted creature obsessed with reclaiming the Ring.
Sauron: The Dark Lord, the main antagonist.

Books of the Trilogy:
1. The Community of the Ring
2. The Two Towers
3. The Return of the King

Tolkien began writing The Lord of the Rings over 12 years ago. 
The book is considered the founding work of modern epic fantasy. 
It was adapted into a film trilogy by Peter Jackson between 2001 and 2003, winning a total of 17 Academy Awards. 
Tolkien also invented complete languages like Elvish (Quenya and Sindarin). 
The book has been translated into over 38 languages and has sold over 150 million copies.
